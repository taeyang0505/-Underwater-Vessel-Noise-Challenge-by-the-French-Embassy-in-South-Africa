New native excerpts: CI01_01, FK01_01 and GR01_01 for training; HI01_01 for
validation; OC03_02 for calibration; SB01_01 reserved as a new sealed assessment
source. Each deployment contributes two distinct dates separated by at least
14 days, for 12 actual contiguous 15-minute excerpts, six stations and 12 dates.
Selection used only published metadata, data availability and native frame
coverage, not model performance or challenge Test characteristics. The previous
OC02_02 independent assessment is not used by this expansion.

Every positive reference is wholly inside a published Good quality interval
covering the exact 25 deci-decade band edges and an explicitly labelled Ship
event. Compromised OC01_01 and low-frequency-compromised MB01_01 were excluded.
The FK01_01 seafloor-contact interval and OC03_02's first-week-November
high-frequency noise-floor warning were also excluded. PM01_01's event file
contains NoShip rather than Ship labels; one acquired excerpt was quarantined
from all positive reference pools. NoShip is not asserted to certify natural
vessel absence.

The public Google Cloud source object generations are pinned. Only exact byte
ranges needed for decoding were downloaded (about 524 MB), not complete source
objects. Therefore a full provider MD5 verification is NOT claimed. Exact range
Content-Range checks, range SHA-256 values, source generation/MD5 metadata,
native audio frames/sample rate and excerpt/array SHA-256 values are recorded.
Native 48/96-kHz mono recordings are retained as genuine 900-second, lossless
crops. All 25 target frequency bands are supported at their native sample rate.
Short source audio is never repeated or interpolated to masquerade as a measured
15-minute passage. Audio conversion uses the released reference convention:
complete two-second Hann periodograms with constant detrending, base-10 band
integration and linear averaging over 30 segments per measured minute.
Digital energies are not claimed to be absolute pressure-calibrated.

Ship annotations indicate events, not isolated-vessel energy or verified hull
independence. Background sounds remain within each added reference. Known
controlled additions provide the energy-fraction targets under the same observed
cell mask. Source station/deployment/day, intervals and byte duplicates are
isolated across locked partitions. SB01_01 has received only preparation and
quality auditing, not model prediction or candidate/calibration selection.
The challenge Test is not used for fitting, distribution tuning, pseudo-labels,
source matching or hidden-target reconstruction. No source audio is publicly
redistributed. This document is a draft only; no additional organizer approval
is claimed.
