External data declaration: VTUAD (rebuilt) v1.3.2

Dataset: VTUAD (rebuilt), version 1.3.2, by Hao Yuan, Wenbo Wang, Tian Li, Guici Chen, Lingjiang Zeng and Sixian Liu.
Public source and download instructions: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23037895
https://zenodo.org/records/23037895
Licence: CC BY 4.0. Attribution to the dataset authors and upstream Ocean Networks Canada recordings: https://data.oceannetworks.ca/ and https://doi.org/10.17616/R3RW43 .
Access: anonymous public HTTPS; no payment or account required. Accessed 24 October 2026. The public deposit includes strict-interval vessel/background WAV archives, metadata, documentation and checksums. Follow its instructions for the split archive files.

Preprocessing: convert strict recorded intervals to the challenge's 25 frequency-band energy summaries using Hann-window PSD integration and linear-energy temporal averaging. Training augmentation includes temporal-envelope/retiming and smooth spectral-response variation, with synthetic additions to the released South African backgrounds in linear energy. Original recording groups are partitioned before augmentation. Reference recordings can include ambient sound; synthetic targets describe the known added reference energy.

No external audio or challenge data are redistributed by this declaration. Test.csv is used only for fixed inference, with no test fitting or hidden-label recovery.
