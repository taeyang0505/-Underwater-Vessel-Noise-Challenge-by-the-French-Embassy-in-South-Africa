External acoustic source declaration — R5 single-CNN pilot

Source: VTUAD (rebuilt), version 1.3.2
Authors: Hao Yuan, Wenbo Wang, Tian Li, Guici Chen, Lingjiang Zeng and Sixian Liu.
Public dataset and access: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23037895
https://zenodo.org/records/23037895
Licence: CC BY 4.0. Credit the VTUAD authors and the upstream Ocean Networks
Canada recordings, https://data.oceannetworks.ca/ and
https://doi.org/10.17616/R3RW43 .
Accessed 24 October 2026 through anonymous public HTTPS without payment or
account credentials. The deposit provides the strict vessel/background WAV
archives, registry, dataset documentation and checksums. Follow the public
deposit instructions to download the split archives and concatenate them before
extracting. No private recording source or prediction API is used.

Preprocessing: use a recorded waveform channel at its native sample rate;
compute complete 2-second Hann periodograms with constant detrending; integrate
25 base-10 deci-decade bands centred at 10^(1.5+0.1*k) Hz, k=0..24. Average
powers in linear energy units over minute supports. Strict recorded intervals
are used. Smooth passage envelopes, bounded retiming and smooth spectral EQ in
synthetic mixtures are declared simulator assumptions, not new recordings or
verified vessel-only signals. The selected source pool contains 119 clips in
22 connected groups; this pilot model uses only its designated fold TRAIN
subset. Groups and duplicate/overlap exclusions are determined before mixing.

Released South African band summaries form the background component. Synthetic
labels retain the known-added-energy definition E=B+alpha*R and
share=sum(O*alpha*R)/sum(O*E), where O is the observed-cell mask. Recorded
reference ambience remains part of the controlled addition; background can
contain natural vessels. Missing observations do not count as silence.

The CNN is trained from scratch; this pilot has no pretrained foundation
weights. The input prior uses only TRAIN data. Any fixed global postprocessing
is selected on our own INNER validation data. Test.csv is used for fixed
per-window inference only; no test fitting, distribution matching, source-clip
matching, pseudo-labelling or hidden-target recovery through leaderboard
probing is performed. No source audio or challenge data are redistributed by
this declaration. This describes the current single-CNN pilot; older
experiments may use other separately documented sources.
