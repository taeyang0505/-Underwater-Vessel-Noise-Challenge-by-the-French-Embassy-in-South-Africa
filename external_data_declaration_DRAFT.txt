Processing and evaluation
One actual waveform channel and native sample rate are used. Complete 2-second
Hann periodograms with constant detrending are integrated over the organizer's
25 base-10 deci-decade bands, centres 10^(1.5+0.1*k), k=0..24. Thirty segment
powers are averaged in linear units for complete measured minutes. Digital gain
is not treated as calibrated pressure. Short excerpts retain their actual length;
surrogate envelopes, retiming, zero extension and smooth channel variation in
the simulator are assumptions, not additional recorded observations.
Synthetic mixtures use released South African observations and permitted
reference energies: E=B+alpha*R. Target share is sum(O*alpha*R)/sum(O*E), with
O marking observed cells. Missing cells never count as silence or target energy.
Backgrounds may contain natural ships. Zero controlled addition is not equivalent
to no naturally present ship. Reference energy includes its recorded ambience.
Reference hull/day and recording-day partitions are formed before mixture
generation, with conflict exclusions and checksum duplicate checks. HearMyShip
has no reliable hull identity, so hull isolation is not claimed for that source.
Optional spectral prototypes aggregate at least three training source groups;
they do not index individual clips or identify challenge examples.
Every South African row is preserved. External weak-teacher scores are recorded
for all 1,715,779 rows, but failed held-out validation and are NOT used as labels.
Test.csv is used only for frozen per-window prediction; no test fitting,
test-distribution tuning, adversarial validation, pseudo-labelling, source-clip
matching, leaderboard probing or hidden-target reconstruction is used.
No raw source audio or challenge recordings are redistributed by this declaration.
